Accessibility statement

Adventurer’s Tome · last updated October 24, 2026

Commitment

Adventurer’s Tome should be usable by every player and Dungeon Master, including people who use a keyboard instead of a mouse, a screen reader, zoom or other assistive technology.

Standard

The site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What the site does to get there

  • Keyboard: every roll, button and menu can be reached with Tab and used with Enter or Space, and whatever has keyboard focus gets a clear gold outline. Escape closes pop-up windows.
  • Screen readers: buttons that are only icons have names, form fields have labels, and pages use headings and landmarks so you can jump around. Roll results, party chat pop-ups and other messages are read out as they appear.
  • Seeing the page: text and controls are checked for color contrast in both light and dark mode, you can switch between the two, and you can zoom the page in your browser or pinch to zoom on a phone.
  • Motion: if your device is set to reduce motion, the site turns off or shortens its animations.
  • Tooltips: icon buttons and rules terms explain themselves when you hover over them.

Technical requirements

The site runs in a current web browser on a computer, tablet or phone, with JavaScript turned on. It doesn’t need any plug-ins, and it relies on standard HTML, CSS and ARIA so it works with the assistive technology built into your device.

How it’s tested

The lobby, party pages and character sheets are tested with the axe accessibility checker in a Chromium-based browser, at desktop and phone sizes, in light and dark mode, and with the keyboard alone.

Known limitations

  • Rearranging the sheet’s panels in Edit layout is done by dragging, which needs a mouse or touch screen. The default layout works without it.
  • Character portraits and party pictures are uploaded by players, so they don’t have written descriptions beyond the character’s or party’s name.
  • The optional Roll20 extension posts rolls into Roll20, whose own accessibility is up to Roll20.

These are tracked as bugs, and each one gets fixed when there’s a way to do it without losing the feature.

Feedback

If something on the site is hard or impossible for you to use, please get in touch through Contact and say which page and what happened. Accessibility problems are treated as bugs and fixed first.

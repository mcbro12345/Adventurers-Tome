Accessibility

Adventurer’s Tome · last updated October 21, 2026

Adventurer’s Tome should be usable by every player, including people who use a keyboard instead of a mouse, a screen reader, zoom or other assistive technology. The goal is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What the site does

  • Keyboard: every roll, button and menu can be reached with Tab and used with Enter or Space, and whatever has keyboard focus gets a clear gold outline. Escape closes pop-up windows.
  • Screen readers: buttons that are only icons have names, form fields have labels, and pages use headings and landmarks so you can jump around. Roll results, party chat pop-ups and other messages are read out as they appear.
  • Seeing the page: text and controls are checked for color contrast in both light and dark mode, you can switch between the two, and you can zoom the page in your browser or pinch to zoom on a phone.
  • Motion: if your device is set to reduce motion, the site turns off or shortens its animations.
  • Tooltips: icon buttons and rules terms explain themselves when you hover over them.

How it’s checked

The lobby, party pages and character sheets are tested with the axe accessibility checker, on desktop and phone sizes and in light and dark mode, and with the keyboard alone.

Known limits

  • Rearranging the sheet’s panels in Edit layout is done by dragging, which needs a mouse or touch screen. The default layout works without it.
  • Character portraits and party pictures are uploaded by players, so they don’t have written descriptions beyond the character’s or party’s name.
  • The optional Roll20 extension posts rolls into Roll20, whose own accessibility is up to Roll20.

Tell us about a problem

If something on the site is hard or impossible for you to use, please contact us and say which page and what happened. Accessibility problems are treated as bugs and fixed first.

Adventurer’s Tome ↔ Roll20 extension

Privacy policy · last updated October 17, 2026

This browser extension connects your Adventurer’s Tome character sheet with your Roll20 game. It is made by the same person who runs adventurers-tome.web.app.

What it does

  • When you roll on your character sheet with “Roll20 rolls for me” turned on, it types that roll into your Roll20 game’s chat and reads the result back to the sheet.
  • When you turn on “Auras follow Roll20 tokens”, it reads the names and positions of the tokens on the Roll20 map page you are viewing, so the sheet can work out who is within range of your aura.
  • It reads the name of your open Roll20 game(s) so you can pick which game to send rolls to.

What it collects and where it goes

  • The extension has no server and collects nothing. It does not track you, does not use analytics, and does not sell or share any data.
  • Roll requests, roll results, game names and token positions are only passed between your own character-sheet tab and your own Roll20 tab, inside your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
  • Rolls you make appear in your Roll20 game’s chat, exactly as if you had typed them, and are handled by Roll20 under its own policies.
  • The extension stores nothing on your computer.

Where it runs

  • On app.roll20.net game pages, to post rolls to chat and read results and token positions.
  • On the Adventurer’s Tome site (adventurers-tome.web.app), a small bridge script stays silent unless the page is a character sheet that says hello to it; it only relays that sheet’s own requests and the answers. It doesn’t run on any other site.

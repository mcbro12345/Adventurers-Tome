Privacy policy

Adventurer’s Tome · last updated October 24, 2026

Adventurer’s Tome runs adventurers-tome.web.app, a free hobby website for D&D 5e character sheets, parties and the DM’s Desk. This page explains what information the site collects, how it’s used, who can see it, and how to get it deleted. The site has no ads and no analytics, and nothing about you is ever sold or shared for marketing.

The information below is used only to run the site and make it better. By using the site, you agree to it being collected and used as this policy describes.

Information we collect and how it’s used

  • Your account: your email address, your name (from Google, or the one you type), your Google profile picture if you sign in with Google, any picture you choose instead, when you joined and when you were last active, and small settings such as which notices you’ve already seen and the order of your parties. This is how the site knows who you are and shows your name to the people you play with.
  • Your characters: everything you put on your sheets, including pictures, and the email addresses of the people you share each one with.
  • Parties: the party’s name and picture, which characters are in it, a summary card for each character (name, class, HP, AC, conditions and the player’s name and picture), the turn order, and invite links (including how many players each link is for and how many have used it).
  • Party chat and whispers: the messages players send each other in a party. They’re deleted automatically about 12 hours after they’re sent and aren’t kept in any log.
  • The DM’s Desk: the sessions, notes, maps, creatures and pictures you keep there.
  • Bug reports: what you write, the page you were on, and your browser’s name and version.
  • Who’s here right now: while you have a sheet open, other people who can open that sheet can see that you’re on it.

Private to the players: party chat, whispers and the DM’s Desk

Some things on the site are kept away from everyone except the people they’re meant for, including the site’s admins. This is enforced by the database’s security rules, not just hidden in the site, and admins have no tool to read them.

  • Party chat can be read only by the players in that party. The party’s Dungeon Master and co-DMs can’t read it, and neither can the site’s admins.
  • Whispers can be read only by the player who sent them and the player they were sent to.
  • Your DM’s Desk can be read only by you. That covers every party’s desk and your shared list of creatures. Nobody else can open it: not your players, not your co-DMs and not the site’s admins. Nothing you do on the Desk goes into the activity log.

Two plain exceptions: if an admin plays in your party, they can read that party’s chat the same way as any other player there. And the site’s owner can technically reach the raw database through Google’s Firebase console, which security rules don’t cover. That console isn’t used to read chats, whispers or anyone’s Desk.

Who can see everything else

  • You can see and change your own account and characters.
  • People you share a character with can view it, or also edit it if you give them edit access.
  • When your character is in a party, the rest of the party sees its summary card, and the party’s Dungeon Master can open the full sheet.
  • The site’s admins can see accounts and characters so they can fix problems and help when something goes wrong. That doesn’t include the chats, whispers and Desks described above.

What anyone with a link can see

  • View-only links: if you turn on a view-only link for a character, anyone with that link can see a read-only copy of the sheet until you turn the link off. The list of people viewing it shows names and pictures of signed-in viewers, never their email addresses.
  • Invite links: when an invite link is pasted into Discord, iMessage and similar apps, its preview shows the party’s name and picture, the Dungeon Master’s name and how many adventurers have joined. Anyone with the link can see that preview.

Log data

  • The site doesn’t use Google Analytics or any other analytics or tracking service.
  • It keeps a short activity log of things like signing in, coming online (and which page you opened), opening, editing, sharing or deleting characters, and party actions. Only the site’s admins can see it, and entries are deleted after 7 days. Chat, whispers and the DM’s Desk are never logged.
  • Like any web server, the services that host the site receive the normal details of each visit, such as your IP address and browser. The site itself doesn’t keep logs of those visits.

Communications

The site doesn’t send newsletters, marketing or promotional emails. The only emails you’ll get are sign-in links you ask for, and a reply if you contact us. News about the site is shown on the site itself.

Cookies and storage on your device

  • The site doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies. Google’s sign-in may set its own cookies.
  • Your browser keeps small settings (like light or dark mode and layout choices), copies of pictures so pages load faster, and the original of a picture you crop, so you can adjust the crop later. These stay on your device.

Services the site uses

  • Google Firebase handles sign-in, the database and the website itself, so your account, characters and Desk are stored on Google’s servers. Signing in with Google, or with an emailed link, goes through Google. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare runs the short invite-link address (invite.adventurerstome.workers.dev), and passes along requests when you import a character from D&D Beyond.
  • YouTube plays the video on the sign-in page, using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced (no-cookie) player. YouTube handles that under its own policies.
  • D&D Beyond: if you import a character from D&D Beyond, the site fetches that character from D&D Beyond.
  • AI on the DM’s Desk (optional): if you add your own API key for an AI provider, the parts of your Desk you choose to use go straight from your browser to that provider, under its own policies. Your key stays in your browser and is never sent to the site. Without a key, nothing is sent anywhere.
  • The optional Roll20 browser extension has its own privacy policy. Rolls sent to Roll20 are handled by Roll20 under its policies.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your account and characters are kept until you delete them, or until you ask for them to be deleted. Your DM’s Desk is kept until you delete what’s in it.
  • You can delete any character you own from its ⋯ menu, and anything on your DM’s Desk from the Desk. Since admins can’t open your Desk, they can’t delete it for you, so clear it yourself before asking for your account to be deleted.
  • To delete your whole account, or to get a copy of your data, contact us. Your account, your characters and your place in any party will be removed. You can also export any of your characters as a file from its sheet, and your Desk notes from the Desk, at any time.

Children

The site isn’t meant for children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and it will be deleted.

Security

Everything is sent over an encrypted connection, you have to sign in to see anything that isn’t behind a link you were given, and the database only lets each person reach what they’re allowed to see. No way of sending or storing information over the internet is completely secure, though, so please don’t put anything on the site you’d need to keep secret.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and any important change is announced on the site. Changes take effect once they’re posted here.

Privacy policy

Adventurer’s Tome · last updated October 21, 2026

Adventurer’s Tome (adventurers-tome.web.app) is a free, hobby website for D&D 5e character sheets and parties. It has no ads and no analytics, and nothing about you is ever sold or shared for marketing. This page explains what the site keeps, who can see it, and how to get it deleted.

What the site keeps when you sign in

  • Your account: your email address, your name (from Google, or the one you type), your Google profile picture if you sign in with Google, any picture you choose instead, when you joined and when you were last active, and small settings such as which notices you’ve already seen and the order of your parties.
  • Your characters: everything you put on your sheets, including pictures, and the email addresses of the people you share each one with.
  • Parties: the party’s name and picture, which characters are in it, a summary card for each character (name, class, HP, AC, conditions and the player’s name and picture), the turn order, and invite links (including how many players each link is for and how many have used it).
  • Party chat and whispers: deleted automatically about 12 hours after they’re sent. They aren’t kept in any log.
  • Activity log: a short record of things like signing in, coming online (and which page you opened), opening, editing, sharing or deleting characters, and party actions. Only the site’s admins can see it, and entries are deleted after 7 days.
  • Bug reports: what you write, the page you were on, and your browser’s name and version.
  • Who’s here right now: while you have a sheet open, other people who can open that sheet can see that you’re on it.

What anyone with a link can see

  • View-only links: if you turn on a view-only link for a character, anyone with that link can see a read-only copy of the sheet until you turn the link off. The list of people viewing it shows names and pictures of signed-in viewers, never their email addresses.
  • Invite links: when an invite link is pasted into Discord, iMessage and similar apps, its preview shows the party’s name and picture, the Dungeon Master’s name and how many adventurers have joined. Anyone with the link can see that preview.

Who can see your things

  • You can see and change your own account and characters.
  • People you share a character with can view it, or also edit it if you give them edit access.
  • When your character is in a party, the rest of the party sees its summary card, and the party’s Dungeon Master can open the full sheet.
  • The site’s admins can see all accounts and characters, so they can fix problems and help when something goes wrong.

Services the site uses

  • Google Firebase handles sign-in, the database and the website itself, so your account and characters are stored on Google’s servers. Signing in with Google, or with an emailed link, goes through Google. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare runs the short invite-link address (invite.adventurerstome.workers.dev). Like any web server it receives the normal details of each visit, such as your IP address; the site doesn’t keep logs of those visits.
  • The optional Roll20 browser extension has its own privacy policy. Rolls sent to Roll20 are handled by Roll20 under its policies.

Stored on your device

  • Your browser keeps small settings (like light or dark mode and layout choices), copies of pictures so pages load faster, and the original of a picture you crop, so you can adjust the crop later. These stay on your device.
  • The site doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies. Google’s sign-in may set its own cookies.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your account and characters are kept until you delete them, or until you ask for them to be deleted.
  • You can delete any character you own from its ⋯ menu.
  • To delete your whole account, or to get a copy of your data, contact us. Your account, your characters and your place in any party will be removed. You can also export any of your characters as a file from its sheet at any time.

Children

The site isn’t meant for children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and it will be deleted.

Keeping things safe

Everything is sent over an encrypted connection, you have to sign in to see anything that isn’t behind a link you were given, and the database only lets each person reach what they’re allowed to see. No system is perfect, though, so please don’t put anything on a character sheet you’d need to keep secret.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and anything important is announced on the site.
